## What is a trade-off ?

A trade-off occurs when improving one architectural quality (pillar) negatively impacts another, requiring a deliberate balance between competing concerns.
## The need for compromise

As mentioned in [the first law of Software Architecture](https://bagerbach.com/books/fundamentals-of-software-architecture/) "Everything in software architecture is a trade-off.", meaning, trade-offs are not failures of design—they are **intentional decisions** aligning architecture with business constraints. 

A system cannot maximize all quality attributes at once; architectural decisions must prioritize certain qualities over others based on business goals and constraints.

However there are design techniques to mitigate the impacts of these trade-offs.
