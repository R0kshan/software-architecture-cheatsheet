Pillars are high-level quality dimensions used to evaluate and guide the design of well-architected systems.
## Quick definitions
#### ⚙️ Operational Excellence
The ability to operate, monitor, anticipate failure and continuously improve systems to deliver business value through process improvement, automation, and incident management.
#### 🔒 Security
Protect data, systems, and assets while enabling authorized access and business operations. 
#### 🔁 Reliability
Ensure a system consistently performs its intended function and recovers quickly from failures.  
#### ⚡ Performance
Efficiently use computing resources to meet system requirements under varying demand.  
#### 🔧 Maintainability
Enable systems to be modified, understood, and evolved with minimal effort and risk.  
#### 💰 Cost Optimization
Deliver maximum business value while minimizing resource usage and expenses.  
#### 🌱 Sustainability
Reduce environmental impact through efficient resource use and long-term planning.
